Studying AI at UP Diliman

The Master of Engineering in Artificial Intelligence is a professional master's degree offered by the UP College of Engineering, launched in August 2025. It is designed to equip students with the knowledge and practical experience to develop and deploy intelligent engineered systems. The program requires 31 units and can be completed in two years full-time or three years part-time — a schedule that fits a working faculty member's life.

Timeline

Why OPM

Original Pilipino Music is more than a genre to me — it's the soundtrack of Filipino identity. OPM grew out of the Manila Sound of the 1970s, which blended Western pop composition with Filipino lyrics, and became a major cultural force after the martial law era ended in 1986. Today it spans ballads, pop, rock, jazz, folk, and rap, and it's the music I reach for most.
